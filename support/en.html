Frequently asked questions

How do I join a round?

Tap the invitation link you received; the app opens with the code filled in. Or open catch., tap "Join game" and enter the code. You can join as long as the round hasn't started yet.

The others don't see me, or the app can't find my location.

  • Allow catch. to use your location "Always" (Android: "Allow all the time") and keep precise location on.
  • On Android: allow notifications, and exclude catch. from battery saving so the phone doesn't pause the app in your pocket.
  • Check your internet connection. Anyone who sends no position for a minute shows up as "signal lost" for the others.

The connection dropped.

catch. reconnects by itself and takes you back into the round, even after you closed the app. If the round is over by then, the app tells you.

How do I delete my data?

catch. has no accounts, and the game server keeps nothing after a round. Your phone only stores your player name and the current round; they disappear when you uninstall the app. More in the privacy policy.

Is it safe to play outdoors?

Always watch out for traffic, stay off private property and play fair. You set the playing field yourselves; pick places where you can move around safely.

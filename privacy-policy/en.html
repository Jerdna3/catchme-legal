1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how catch. ("the app") handles your data. catch. is an app for real-world games: you play together outdoors, for example tag or capture the flag, and the app shows on the map where the others are.

Summary

  • You don't need an account. You only enter a player name of your choice.
  • While you are in a round, the app sends your location to our game server. It only passes it on to the players of your round, and only as far as the rules of the game allow.
  • The game server stores nothing permanently: everything is kept in memory only and disappears shortly after the round ends.
  • The camera image and the motion sensors are only processed on your phone. Nothing is recorded or sent.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics services, no sale of data.

2. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is the developer and publisher of catch., based in Hamburg, Germany.

Contact: pascalnuescheler@gmail.com

3. What the app does

Someone creates a round in the app and gets a short code (for example K7QF). Anyone with the code or the invitation link can join the round as long as it hasn't started yet. During the round, the players see each other on the map: depending on the game and their role live, only now and then, or only nearby. In the camera view, the app shows game objects such as treasures, tools or the flag in the camera image. Our game server checks whether a move counts, so nobody can cheat.

4. What data is processed

a) Location

  • From the moment you create or join a round until you leave it, the app regularly determines your precise location and sends it to our game server: coordinates, accuracy and the time of the measurement.
  • This also happens while the app runs in the background or the screen is off; otherwise the game wouldn't work with the phone in your pocket. On Android, a persistent notification shows that catch. is sharing your location. Outside a round, the app does not use your location.
  • The game server passes your location only to the players of the same round, and only as the rules of the game provide. For example, in tag the runners see the catchers only every 5 minutes, and in the APV Game the game makers see all teams. Positions that someone is not allowed to see never leave the server.
  • The server also uses your location to check moves, for example whether you were close enough to catch someone. It also computes warnings such as "catcher nearby"; the players warned only learn that someone is close, not where.
  • Your phone's operating system determines the location (Android through Google Play services, iPhone through Apple's Location Services). Their settings and terms apply to that.

b) Player name and game data

  • You choose your player name yourself; it doesn't have to be your real name. The players of your round see it.
  • During a round, the server processes the game data: your color, role and team, points, who caught whom, collected objects and tools, staked-out playing fields and territories, and in the APV Game the team codes, entered code guesses and the announcements of the game makers.
  • Please don't put personal data (such as an address or phone number) into names or announcements.

c) Camera and motion sensors

  • In the camera view, the app shows game objects and players in the camera image. For this it uses your phone's camera, motion sensor and compass.
  • The camera image and sensor data are processed only on your phone. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent. The app does not use the microphone.
  • When you tap a target in the camera view, the app only sends which target you mean. The server checks with your location whether it counts.

d) Data on your phone

  • The app stores on your phone your player name, the round you are currently in (its code and an identifier so you can return after restarting the app), and whether you want to see the map as a satellite image.
  • You delete this data by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app. The app deletes the stored round itself when you leave the round.

e) Invitation links

  • An invitation contains a link with the code of the round. Whoever opens it calls up a page on our game server. As with any web page, the server receives the IP address, the browser or device type and the language setting in order to show the right page. The page sets no cookies and contains no tracking services.
  • Messengers such as WhatsApp may call up the page themselves to show a preview of the link.

f) Technical data

  • With every connection to the game server, technically necessary data such as your IP address is processed to establish and secure the connection.
  • For the map, the app loads map data from Google Maps. Google receives technically necessary data such as the IP address, device information and the map section shown. Google's map component also reports crash data, diagnostics (for example the device model and Android version), how the map is panned and zoomed, and a random identifier of the component. According to Google, this serves the stability and improvement of its services, not advertising.
  • For updates, the app checks with Expo's update service. It transmits the platform (Android or iPhone), the app version, the installed update version and a random identifier of the installation, which Expo uses for statistics on how widely updates have spread.

Legal basis: We process this data to provide the game you want to play (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and on the basis of our legitimate interest in secure, fair and working gameplay and in updates of the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). In addition, you allow access to location and camera yourself in your phone's settings and can withdraw it there at any time.

5. Service providers and recipients

Apart from the players of your round, only these service providers, which we need to run the app, receive data:

  • Render (Render Services, Inc., USA) runs our game server in a data center in Frankfurt am Main (EU). Location, player name, game data and the invitation pages go through it. Render's privacy policy
  • Google (Google Maps) displays the map, see section 4 f. Your use of Google Maps is subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA) delivers the app's updates, see section 4 f. Expo's privacy policy
  • Google Play and Apple (App Store, TestFlight) distribute the app. They provide us with aggregated statistics on installs and crashes; through TestFlight we also receive crash reports and the feedback you send yourself. The privacy policies of Google and Apple apply.

These service providers are based in the USA, so data may also be transferred there. According to the providers, such transfers rely on recognized safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or the Data Privacy Framework; details are in their privacy policies.

6. Retention

  • Game server: location, player name and game data are kept in memory only, not in a database. They disappear as soon as nobody is in the round any more. If someone loses the connection, the server keeps their seat until the end of the running round, otherwise for 5 minutes. A round lasts at most 24 hours, and every restart of the server deletes everything.
  • Logs: our server writes neither locations nor game data to logs. The hosting provider Render may keep technical logs of requests for a short time.
  • On your phone: until you change the data, leave the round or uninstall the app (see section 4 d).

7. No account, no ads, no tracking

  • The app needs no account, no email address and no phone number. It does not access contacts, photos or files.
  • It contains no ads and no analytics or tracking services (no Google Analytics, no Firebase, no Crashlytics or similar).
  • We don't sell data or pass it on for advertising.

8. Permissions at a glance

Android

  • Location (precise and approximate): your position in the game, see section 4 a.
  • Background location, foreground service and notifications: so the game keeps running with the screen off; the notification shows that catch. is sharing your location.
  • Camera: camera view, see section 4 c.
  • High sampling rate motion sensors: so game objects stay steadily in place in the camera image.
  • Vibration: proximity alarm, for example when a catcher is nearby.
  • Internet and network state: connection to the game server, the map and updates.
  • Further technical permissions (for example keeping the device awake or resuming background tasks after a restart) are needed by Android components to run the app. No data is collected through them.

iPhone

  • Location Services ("While Using the App" and "Always"), Camera and Motion & Fitness, for the same reasons as on Android.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in your phone's settings. Without location you can't play, though, and without the camera the camera view is missing.

9. Data security

The connection to the game server is encrypted (TLS). The server decides by the rules of the game who gets which positions; the app only receives what it is allowed to show. Anyone who knows the code of a round can join it as long as it hasn't started yet, so only share the code and the invitation link with people you want to play with. No software can guarantee absolute security; we limit data processing to what the game needs.
